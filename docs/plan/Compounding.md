# Compounding: how each cycle makes Ardan greater

The owner, Oct 28: "It's not just the bugs and fixes. It's compounding improvements on how this game can be as great as possible."

Every study, improve and repeat cycle now has two halves:
- **Fix**: what the reviewers found broken.
- **Grow**: new capabilities that make the game more worth playing, replaying and sharing. Each one is built so the next cycle can build on it.

## The pillars

Each new piece should strengthen at least one pillar, and ideally feed another.

| Pillar | The question it answers | Built so far | Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A world worth exploring** | Why go anywhere? | The Great Continent (99), its peoples, wars and beasts (990–993), wilds (997) | Journeys with stops; a fogged atlas of places known and heard of (9990–9991, cycle 1 grow) |
| **A life that is yours** | Who am I, and what am I becoming? | Ambitions (97), confidants (999), Hall of Lives (995) | Arms, motto and named companions; inner voices; chapters with aims; thoughts that ripen (9992–9993) |
| **Moments worth showing** | What do I tell my friends? | Cut-ins, duels, the farewell screen (96, 48, 28) | Share cards made from any moment (9994) |
| **A world that answers** | Does what I do matter? | NPC initiative, beliefs, schemes, wars (21, 64, 39, 09) | A trade life on the Silk Road (9995); careers past 30 (994) |
| **Replay and mastery** | Why play again? | The Hall's unlocks and kept endings (995) | Dynasty legacies, seeded weekly challenges |
| **Beauty** | Does it look like a masterpiece? | The Woodblock & Light brief (91–96) | More portrait poses, a ¾ head, parallax title, battle clashes |

## The rules

- **Leave a hook.** Every feature exposes data or a registry that others can read or add to: sites, places known, identity tokens, goods, aims. The next cycle then adds content instead of plumbing.
- **Measure first.** Each grow lane reports a number before and after: places found, distinct events, coin against sinks, shares made, replay unlocks.
- **One coherent world.** Nothing is faked for the far lands, and nothing is simulated at a lower fidelity.
- **Reviewers judge growth too.** Cycle 2's reviewers judge what the cycle 1 growth makes possible, as well as what it broke.

## Free rein (owner, Oct 28)

"Growth doesn't have to be new. Can be improved graphics, better UI and animations, more detail. Backtests on the most well designed or popular games and see what can be taken from those. You have free rein to edit in whatever way you want. A full political system. Detailed wars done to the last unit. Complex systems. Beautifully graphic design and motion graphics. Whatever is necessary."

How this changes the lanes:
- **Growth includes polish.** Lane G covers motion, portraits, the title, the battle screen and fine detail everywhere.
- **Backtests guide the big systems.** Lane R studies about fifteen of the best-designed games, finds the specific mechanisms behind them, and writes a ranked list of what to bring into Ardan in `docs/research/backtests.md`.
- **Two big systems are queued after the cycle 1 fixes are integrated:**
  - Lane P: a full political system, with offices, laws, councils, factions, elections, vassal contracts and legitimacy.
  - Lane WD: war down to the unit, with companies, named officers, formations, morale per unit, supply and orders.
