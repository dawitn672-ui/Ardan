# Ardan v2.0.0-beta.1: narrative director, worldbuilder and consistency QA

**How I tested.** I read every culture, name list, realm, rank and title table (`LORE.cultures`, `LORE.realms`, `LORE.titlesByRealm`, `GD.MIL`, `GD.NOBLE_RUNGS`, `A.COUNCIL`, `src/v2/names.js`, `47-realm-bible`, `99994-v2-family`, `99995-v2-nobility`, `99999-v2-names` and `9997-companies`). Then I ran these headless on `/tmp/ardan/snap/index.html`:

- **A birth census:** 48 newborns, one per realm and estate across 8 realms and 6 estates (`world/out/census.json`).
- **A court census** of all 35 realms (`out/court.json`).
- **Five career probes** that move a soldier up rank by rank, make him Marshal and then resign (`out/career*.json`, `out/ranks2.json`).
- **A stale-office check** on 3 years of one world (`out/occ.json`).
- **A consistency sweep:** 12 worlds, 30 years each, with 60 invariants checked every 5 years. The lives cover newborn and adult starts, human, orc, elf and dwarf, and peasant to royal, about 2,400 cards in all. Scripts are `world/sweeplib.js` and `world/agg.mjs`; results are in `out/life-*.json` and `out/agg-all.txt`. Screenshots are in `world/shots/`.

The ids below come from those runs; the number in braces is the seed. The map of where posts are stored and the Marshal reproduction in `/tmp/ardan/posts-map.md` agree with these findings, and I do not repeat them here.

**Verdict.** The world keeps no single record of who holds what. A post can live in up to six places at once:

- a council seat (`r.council`)
- a court title (`A.S.court`)
- a shire office (`r.pol.off`)
- a company slot (`d.bs[].p`)
- two free-text strings: `p.title`, frozen from the lore, and `p.occ`, written by whoever appointed the person

No feature clears another feature's store. Every symptom in the owner's complaint 2 reproduces on demand. The same root cause also gives counties held by dead men for 27 years, a 2-year-old Chancellor, two Doges, and a grandfather who is heir to his own daughter.

## Keep
- **The non-European name pools are well researched, not generic:** Tibetan Skyfolk, Amharic Abari, Bedouin Sandfolk (Dughaym, Mutlaq), Egyptian Tamari, Kazakh and Mongol Wind Riders, Swahili Keshi.
- **`A.V2.kin.relationLabel` gives exact terms whenever the tree is complete:** "Uncle by marriage", "Father (at odds with you)", "First cousin (your enemy)".
- **`A.rulerStyle` genders the ruler's style on screen.**
- **The `takeSeat` path is the right model:** the displaced holder becomes a rival.
- **The text layer is clean.** In about 2,400 cards there were no raw tokens and only 4 real pronoun slips. One child had a job (an apprentice at 15), and there were no sibling marriages and no human parent younger than their child.
- **The realm bible's customs, house words and arms** give the realms real texture.
