# Ardan v2.0.0-beta.1: senior game designer's critique (systems, core loop, pacing, progression, careers, war, relationships, goals)

**How I played.** Three lives through the real UI with Playwright: creation clicks, **Next month** (Next week on campaign), activity tiles, Path and job buttons, every card answered by a persona that reads the stakes; state read with `page.evaluate`.

- **L1 Godric of Kingsmere:** a free peasant boy, born January 1105 in Aldmark. Played from birth for 19 years 8 months, until he was killed at 19 by the "Caravan guard" trap (#3).
- **L2 Aelfric Fairrush:** a younger son of a noble house of Aldmark, enlisted at 16. Played for 20 years, to age 36: Squad Captain at 16, Commander at 29, 76,529 suns.
- **L3 Orsino of Novice:** a merchant's son, 22, in the Free City of Ostra. Played for 19 years, until marsh fever killed him at 41 (#11).

That came to 465 cards, about 730 clock steps and 0 page errors. Two of the three lives were ended by the game, just short of 20 years. Three experiments:

- **E3** (L2's save): the rank ladder in peace and in war, then resigning.
- **E4** (L2's save): a General's and a Captain's war.
- **Speed:** render cost on saves of different ages.

Evidence is in `/tmp/ardan/critique/design/`: `L1..L3/log.jsonl`, `shots/`, `e3-ranks*.json`, `e4-gen/`, `e4-cap/` and `bench-*.json`.

## Flaws, most severe first

| # | Flaw (concrete: on-screen text, screen, function or file) | Severity /10 | How to fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A promotion does not hand over command, and leaving a post changes nothing** (complaint 2, confirmed). E3 ran the game's own promotion steps (`rank++`, the council seat at rank 9, `A.wsAttachPC`). From Captain to Marshal the Path reads "1st Yewmarch Spears · leads 200,000", and `pc.mil.unit` never changes. In war, rank 7 takes "First Army of Aldmark" (the realm's only army) from Garrick Harrow, the sitting Marshal, with one log line. At rank 9, `council.marshal` is overwritten and Harrow gets no scene, post or word. As Marshal in wartime, `A.resign()` succeeds, since ranks 6 and up may leave mid-war. Afterwards the First Army's general is still the player and `council.marshal` still names him (`e3-ranks-war.json`). | 10 | First-class posts (`S.posts`: Marshal, each army's general, each division's commander), one holder each. Promotion is an appointment scene to a vacancy, or a named transfer of the old holder. Resigning empties the post, names a successor and costs honour and regard (in war, desertion at any rank). Unit and command text come from the post, never from `rank`. |
| 2 | **At birth your relationships are the whole extended family, in every class** (complaint 5). A newborn starts with 17–29 relationships, all of them kin. For seed 11, the serf, peasant, artisan, merchant and gentry starts are identical: father, mother, 1 sibling, 3 grandparents, 5 uncles, 5 in-laws, 8 cousins (`rel-at-start.json`). The People tab says "These are the people you have chosen to know" above "FAMILY (24)". Each is "Close" or "Friendly" and "Wants your company" of a newborn (`shots/people-peasant-born-1366.png`). In 42 starts (7 classes × 2 ages × 3 seeds) no relation is anything but kin: no nurse, maid, tutor or guard knight for any noble or royal. The skill `life-sim-systems` §4 still prescribes walking the tree 3 generations. | 9 | The tree holds kin you know *of*; `pc.rel` holds people you *know*, seeded by household: commoner = parents, siblings, a grandparent under the roof; noble = + wet-nurse, nurse, tutor at 6, guard knight, groom; royal = + household officers. Uncles and cousins enter only through a scene (visit, funeral, wedding, fostering). Fix the skill so the next lane doesn't rebuild the flood. |
| 3 | **Cards hide lethal fights behind mild words.** L3's "Caravan guard" offered "Go forward to meet them / Starts a fight / +25 suns". It opened "Ambush … to the death" against a Veteran, and he was "left for dead" with a lasting head wound. The safer-looking alternative read "If it fails: hurt", so a careful reader picks the deadly one (my persona did). The same card killed L1 at 19 ("You: Veteran … You should win this", then 14 rounds and "the light is going"), and caught L2 as a division Commander. L2's "The toll fort" offered "Search the cart … If it fails: a fight", which led to "the toll fort gate · night · to the death". "Caravan guard" came up in all three lives. Of the 19 "Starts a fight" choices I saw, none mentioned death. | 8 | Compute the stake from the duel the choice will open ("a fight to the death against a veteran: could kill you"). Fail any event whose `fx` starts a lethal fight without saying so in a linter. |
| 4 | **The game slows the longer you play** (complaint 1, confirmed at the page). Measured back to back on one page: one `UI.refresh` takes 1.2–1.5 s on a new game and 2.1–3.2 s at 15 years (L1, 1120), and a 2-card month made 10 refresh calls (25 s wall time). DOM nodes went from 1,091 to 1,728, story text 5.6×, the save 2.7 to 4.0 MB; the engine's month alone did not grow. The cost is redrawing the whole page several times a step, with an ever-longer Story. | 8 | Render incrementally. Keep only the current year in the Story DOM (older years as one collapsed line each). Do one refresh per card close, not per slice. Add a perf budget test: one refresh at 30 years under 150 ms. |
| 5 | **Battle choices at your rank ignore the battle** (complaints 10 and 11). In `99997` b-battle.core.js:205, `W.choices = b => VW.CHOICES[b.rank]`, so the list never changes. At Urgol (a mountain pass, facing "the 5th Gorath Horse") and at Evenglade (hills, facing levies), the Squad Captain got exactly "Keep the squad tight / Plug the gap in the line / Pick off stragglers" in every phase: Skirmish, Clash, Press and Decision. Each phase reports "Your side loses a handful, theirs a handful". | 8 | Generate each phase's 3 choices from arm × ground × enemy type × phase × weather. Spears against horse: "ground the butts and brace". Archers in rain: "keep the strings dry". Scouts: "slip round their flank". Decision: pursue, hold or loot. Keep `CHOICES` only as the fallback. |
| 6 | **Enlisting is one free click and a toast; your arm of service is cosmetic and comes late.** The toast: "You take the coin … Squad Captain among the spearmen of the 2nd Company, 1st Yewmarch Spears". He was a 16-year-old Journeyman lancer, in "the heaviest cavalry in the Westland". His court post ("Squire of the court") disappears without a word. "Choose your arm of service" came a year later, after two battles as a spearman, and the pick changes no battle option (see #5). | 7 | Make enlisting a muster of 2–3 scenes: branch from birth, gear and skill (lance → men-at-arms, bow → archers, woodcraft → scouts), the oath, a first post under a named superior. The arm is fixed then and changed only by transfer, with its own missions and battle choices. |
| 7 | **Events invent family.** L1 is an only child (in the save, his parents' only child is Godric). Yet "Covering for your sibling" says "Your sister Sunniva is swimming in the forbidden millrace" (`lf_sib_cover`: `setPid(p, ctx, sibOf(p))`, with no sibling check in cond). The event created "Sunniva of Kingsmere", born 1111 with no parents, and filed her as his sibling in 1115, so she was 4 in the millrace. His parents' children list still names only him. L2 has no siblings, yet gets "your brother Hamo Carrholt", with another house's surname, in two events ("The favorite" ×2); by 1125 a sibling sits in his relations. L1 is told "Ysolde, who you played with every day" has died, though they never met. | 7 | Gate every kin event on the real relative (`C.family`). If the relative is missing, `setPid` drops the card; it never mints or borrows a person. Add a lint rule: a kin token without the matching helper in cond fails. |
| 8 | **A life from birth is mostly empty clicks.** In L1, ages 0–12 took 155 presses of Next month, and 129 of them (83%) brought no card. Ages 0–4 took 59 presses, 55 of them empty ("A month of milk and sleep." for two years). Under the owner's rule (Next month only), a birth start is a chore before the first real fork, "Bound for seven years", at 13. | 7 | Keep the one button, but in infancy and childhood let it run on to the next card (at most to the end of the season). Show the quiet months as a strip, not as presses. |
| 9 | **War and private life ignore where you are.** L2's company "holds Grakzag Oasis" in Gorath, yet every card is headed "Yewmarch" (his home). A Squad Captain on campaign jousts abroad at "The Great Tournament of Irondeep" (August 1105). In E4, a General besieging Brannoch's walls gets "Above, or below … Stolen hours by the river". His father counsels him at the roll call after a battle ("Come home whole"). | 7 | Track the player's location (camp, march, siege, home) and filter pools by it; on campaign draw only camp, march and front scenes; a gathering needs leave, at a cost. |
| 10 | **A former Marshal starts again as a clerk.** After resigning (E3), the Path shows "Without a post". `pc.veteran` (rank 9) is used only by the doors list, which offers a former Marshal what it offers an old sergeant ("Bounty hunter: Old sergeants make good thief-takers"), beside "Enlist … Start in the ranks" and "Clerk & office: start as Copyist · 60 suns/yr". | 7 | Map rank to lateral posts (Marshal → councillor, castellan, order commander; Captain → watch captain, master-at-arms). Keep the style and a pension. Veterans receive offers; they don't pick from a menu. |
| 11 | **Death comes without warning, and the epitaph is someone else's.** L3 died "of marsh fever" at 41 on 1 January 1124. His health read 100 ("hale") the January before and still reads "hale" on the death screen. There was no illness card, no treatment choice and no last scene, though 49-body-mind promises illness over months with treatment choices. The banker's ending is "Master of the Craft … Somewhere a door still hangs true because of it." L1, killed at 19, is awarded "Survivor". | 7 | Fatal illness runs as an arc (onset card, worsening, treatment, last wishes) visible in health. Endings read the career the life actually had. |
| 12 | **The campaign between battles is empty clicking.** On campaign, Next month becomes Next week. L2 spent 27 of his first 47 steps on weeks, 21 of them with nothing to answer. E4's General read "A week passes. Your host sits before enemy walls." five weeks running, and a Captain read "A week passes. Your host is on the march." seven weeks running, with no card. Supply, scouting, foraging and attrition live only in the separate War Room, which the loop never prompts. | 6 | Make each campaign week a decision from the army's state (forage or press on, scout the ford, send for the carts, assault or starve); skip weeks with nothing to decide. |
| 13 | **Peace freezes the army ladder.** L2 sat at Captain from 1107 to 1115 ("Months at your present rank 79 / 16" in 1114) with every hard gate met ("Command skill: Master, enough", "Battles seen 4 / 3", literate). The only row still open was "A deed that stands out: nothing above the ordinary". Renown faded from 43 to 33. His Path counts 4 battles in 20 years of service, all in 1105–06, and nothing pointed him to a war elsewhere, an order or a transfer. | 6 | Let peace service count (garrison commands, real border patrols, war games, a foreign contract or an order's trials), with a floor on the board's chance for long service. |

## What works (keep)

- **The Edda arc.** L2 hands his aunt to the watch at 17; years later come "What Edda told", "Back from the hulks" and "Why Edda Fairrush talked". A real consequence chain from one choice.
- **Merchant cards.** "A share in a ship", "Cheap grain, hungry spring", "A knight short of silver" and "The guild elects its wardens" are real trade-offs with readable odds.
- **"After Urgol"** sets three accounts side by side (yours, the captains', the chronicler's) and lets you choose which to tell.
- **The commander's battle.** Wing tactics gated by drill ("Never drilled, so not offered: Stake Line"), a real reserve, and an honest after-action report ("Your line broke first, in the seventh push").
- **Thoughts that ripen into traits** ("Every coin is a seed" becomes Ambitious).
- **Staged company missions** ("Bandit hunt": three stages, skill tags, named men).
- **The childhood voice.** It is dry, concrete and unsentimental.

## Top 5 to do first

1. **Make posts real** (#1, #10, #22, #23). Create `S.posts` with one holder each. Promotion is an appointment scene and resignation a vacancy with consequences. All rank text is read from the post, and veterans get lateral posts.
2. **Relationships start with the household** (#2, #7, #19, #20). Kin stay in the tree; household and staff go in the list. People enter through scenes. No event may name kin that don't exist. Correct the `life-sim-systems` skill too.
3. **Honest stakes and previews** (#3, #17). Build each card's stake from the `fx` and the duel it will open, and add a linter and a replay test so the card can't lie.
4. **War by arm and ground** (#5, #6, #12, #29). Make the muster a scene that fixes your arm. Generate rank choices from arm, terrain, enemy and phase. Make campaign weeks into decisions about supply, scouting and assault.
5. **Speed and context** (#4, #8, #9, #14, #15). Render incrementally with a bounded Story. Filter events by location, age and calendar month. Let childhood's quiet months run on to the next card.
