Gate 2 · character art · for the owner's eye

Ardan · the people

Clean line with soft tone, after the owner's two reference sheets: full figures with their own stance and build, every piece of dress and armour its own drawn layer, and faces set by hand. Nothing here is a picture file. Each person is a short list of numbers and items, drawn by the parts library when the page opens.

The lineup

Fifteen people, drawn to one scale. The commons: a bondman, a cottar's child, a tenant's wife, a smith, a wool factor. Cloister, levy and court: a priest, a man-at-arms, a knight, an earl's daughter, the king. Under arms: an outrider, a lady knight, a serjeant, a sellsword and a northern housecarl, each in a harness of their own.

Profile cards

The same drawings, closer, on the card the game would show: name, estate and rank, a line about the person, and plain measures in words.

The face and build maker

Every feature is set by hand and the picture redraws as you drag. Start from anyone in the lineup, or roll a stranger. Sliders carry words at their ends, never numbers.

Face code

One face, four ages

Growth and age are part of the drawing, not a filter: a child's large skull and short face, the jaw and brow that come in with the years, then lines, hollows, thinner lips, grey, and a little lost height.

Gear as layers: make and rarity

Dress, armour and arms are separate items put on in order: every piece of a harness is its own layer. Each piece of arms carries two measures, its make and its rarity. A library of hundreds of weapons would be more rows of the same data.

What it costs to draw

Measured in this browser when the page opened, across the fifteen people of the lineup.

Compose is the parts library turning a look into SVG. Paint is the browser turning that SVG into pixels, timed by drawing it to a canvas at twice the size shown. In the game a portrait would be painted once and kept as an image.

Source: docs/design/demo-2d/gate2/. The same look always gives the same drawing: the parts take their dice from a seeded stream named by the person's id, never from the clock.
