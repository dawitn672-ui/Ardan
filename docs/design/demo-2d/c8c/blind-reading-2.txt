**Hard to tell apart:**
- **Serf and peasant:** Both wear plain undyed tunics with rope belts. I split them mostly on small extras like a hat, a neckerchief or a sash, so I may have some wrong.
- **Merchant and knight or noble:** Both wear rich cloth, and what separated them was a belt pouch with keys against a sword or gold court trim.
- **Soldier and knight:** Both wear helmets and mail. I called the fuller harness with a sword and bracers (8, 25) knights, and the shield or bow carriers soldiers.
- **Royal and noble:** I used a crown as the deciding sign. The fur-collared figures without crowns (3, 12, 15, 30) were the closest calls.
- **Artisan:** The apron was the clear sign. The artisans were easy to spot except for 32, whose apron sits over finer clothes.
