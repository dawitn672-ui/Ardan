The hardest split was serf versus peasant. Both wear plain, undyed or single-colour tunics with rope or cloth belts. I separated them mainly by cloth. Rough, bleached, shapeless shifts went to serf (17, 21, 23, 25). Dyed, better-cut tunics went to peasant (8, 13, 29). Those calls are weak. Knight or noble was also hard to separate from royal and merchant. Crown and ermine made royalty easy. But 6 and 10 wear similar draped, gold-trimmed clothes, which could be noble or wealthy merchant. Merchants and nobles share fur collars, so I used the purse and keys to mark merchants (3, 16, 24, 32). Soldier versus knight depended on plate armour and heraldry (9) against padded sleeves and an open kettle helmet (7, 14, 20). Clergy were easy because of the habits and stoles.
