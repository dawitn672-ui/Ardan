The hardest pairs to separate were serf and peasant, merchant and noble, and artisan and merchant. Serf and peasant both wear undyed rough cloth with rope belts. I went by how patched, drab and dark the cloth was (9, 18 as serfs), against lighter and cleaner cloth (10, 19, 24 as peasants). Those calls are the least certain. Merchant and noble overlap in dyed colours and jewellery, so I used the belt pouch and keys for merchants, and fur, chains and circlets for nobles. People 4, 11 and 14 are the least sure of those. Artisan and merchant differ mainly in the apron and tools, which is a clear cue. Soldier and knight were easy to tell apart, because plate harness is rare and common soldiers wear a helmet, tabard and shield. Clergy were easy to spot from the stole, cowl or wimple. Royals were easy to spot from the crowns.
