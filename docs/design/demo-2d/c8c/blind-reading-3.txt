The stations that were hardest to tell apart:
- **Serf and peasant (10, 18, 19, 21, 24):** Both wear undyed, plain tunics with rope belts. I only separated them by the patch on the chest and by how drab the cloth was. 24 and 21 are the least certain calls.
- **Noble, merchant and royal (4, 11, 14, 22, 29):** All wear fur and gold. I decided between them on whether there was a crown, a sword, or a belt pouch or keys. 22 has a crown but a less elaborate cloak, so it could be a noble.
- **Clergy and peasant (3, 32):** Grey hooded cowls look much like a peasant's cloak. Only the pendant, stole or rosary tipped it to clergy.
- **Artisan and merchant (5, 7, 23):** Aprons and tools made the artisans easy. 5 has no clear marker, so merchant is a weak guess.
- **Soldier:** Easy to tell apart in every case, because of the helm or shield, the mail collar and the parti-coloured tunic.
