**Hard to tell apart:** The serfs and peasants were the hardest. Both wear plain undyed cloth with rope or simple belts. I separated them by patches and sackcloth (serf) versus a slightly better cut, an apron or a shawl (peasant), so 21, 24 and 29 are guesses. Nobles and merchants overlap too: 11, 14 and 6 all have fine cloth and gold trim. I treated pouches and keys as the mark of a merchant, and a sword, chains or fur as the mark of a noble. Artisans and peasants both wear aprons, so I counted a visible tool as the deciding sign. Royals and high nobles are close, and I relied on crowns. The clergy and soldiers were the clearest, because stoles and crosses or helmets and shields stand out.
