Ardan · ten legendary arms

Ten pieces from ten realms and peoples, each in its own people’s craft and no two alike. The first is drawn after the owner’s example of a legendary blade and is Aldmark’s. None of them glows: what marks a legend is its metal, its making and what has happened to it. For each, the piece itself with two closer looks; who made it and what it has done; who owns it, who bears it and why; the person who has it, drawn by the game’s figure library with the piece on the figure; and, to one scale, the ordinary arm of its kind. Nothing on this page is a picture file. Every piece is a record of parts, drawn when the page opens.

The same ten at the size of a list

The figure and the piece on it, drawn small and without the light pass, as a list of people would show them: outline, metal, one accent, enough to know the piece.
