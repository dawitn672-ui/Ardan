# Ardan v3: the living world (the owner's design)

*Opened 2026-11-13 07:21 ET (D-037). v2 is the owner's complaint list of Nov 12 (`docs/V2_SPEC.md`, `docs/CRITIQUE.md`); v3 is the
new design the owner set on Nov 13 in orders 28 to 33 (`docs/OWNER_ORDERS.md`). Every rule of v2 still holds.*

## What v3 is
A world that runs itself and people who differ deeply: markets that move by their own causes, an armoury of hundreds
of weapons and armour pieces with a few legendary treasures, and growth in depth: more stats, thousands of skills and
hundreds of fighting styles. Realistic, but fun for the player at all times (order 31).

## The owner's rules for v3
1. **Markets** are as realistic as possible: one per settled province; prices move by named causes (D-032 §1).
2. **Goods:** 40 or more, real goods a person uses, not only materials (D-032 §2).
3. **Merchants are met by standing:** a duke summons them in days, a baron waits longer, a nameless commoner cannot
   seek them (D-032 §3).
4. **Income follows the economy only through what you make and sell** (D-032 §4).
5. **Money is as deep as realism needs:** loans, crown debts and defaults, thinned coin (D-032 §5).
6. **Every item has a rarity** (common, rare, epic, legendary) **and a quality of make** (crude to masterwork); the
   smith's skill, the material, the forge and luck decide (D-032 §6).
7. **Armour is drawn piece by piece** on the figure (D-032 §7).
8. **Legendaries** are near-indestructible heirlooms and national treasures that outclass ordinary gear, each with a
   signature art and its own finer design; skill still decides most fights and owning one draws danger (D-032 §8, D-034).
9. **More stats**, each clearly defined: what it does, how it rises and falls, its limits (D-034).
10. **Skills in the thousands**, passive and active, for every route in depth; proficiencies merged in; skills level,
    evolve and merge (D-034).
11. **Fighting styles in the hundreds:** each nation's standard training per regiment with its tactics, great and
    knightly families' styles, elite orders' styles, some weaker than the standard and some far beyond (D-034).
12. **The character art** is the approved gate 2 direction (D-035), and the Profile tab shows the person as a
    game-like page (order 33; built in v2 as work item P, grown in v3).
