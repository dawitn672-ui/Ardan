# Reasons for war and the cracks in great powers (from the old Codex)

*Stamped 2026-11-16 · research lift, plain design text, no code · source: `docs/research/ardan-codex-old.html` (compendium
ch. 4, 9; atlas §5 realm entries and §6; chronicle). Feeds R1 / W1-2 (`war.md` §11), W1-6 (§13), the Known World's lore.
Ids (POL-, EVT-, MN-, CK-) are the Codex's; they name Far Lands things, so use the **patterns**, and re-skin them to the
Known World's 34 realms (the Far Lands are out of the game, V2_SPEC complaint 3).*

## 1 · The pattern every Codex realm carries

Each of the 118 realm entries answers the same questions. The game's realm record (`00-lore.json`) has pay-like fields
(`taxes`, `debts`, `levy`) but no want, fear, secret or brittleness. These six are what R1 needs as inputs.

| Field | Meaning | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Dynasty and age** | A dynasty over about 120 years is *brittle*: elites rule through it (67 of the 118 are marked so) | the throne's right, §13.1 |
| **Pay** | How soldiers are paid, which "decides how it would fall": land held for service (21), tolls and tribute (18), hired or monthly coin (15), levy only (4) | the army pressure, §10.4, §13.1 |
| **Wants** | A standing want of the realm or its ruler | a reason *for* an option, §11.1 |
| **Fears** | A standing fear | a reason *against*, or fear-struck first blow |
| **Custom** | One law or habit that binds the ruler too | a constraint that rules some options out |
| **Secret** | A fact someone is hiding, with who knows it | a lever; a reason a plot or a war starts when it comes out |

## 2 · How great powers hold together, and the crack in each

One idea holds each, and the idea has a named crack. These are ready-made *pressures* for §13.1.

| Holds by | The crack | Reasons it produces |
|---|---|---|
| **The Ledger state** (Taulen, Hesh): count, tax and examine everyone; officials by exam | **Money.** Half-notes written 3 for every 2 recorded, now passing at 60 in 100; the army eats two thirds of revenue; borrowing against next year's silk. Hesh: conquerors turned soft, banner princes hiring substitutes (one in three on Archery Day 1103) | arrears, mutiny, a debased coin, a war to take the north before the dynasty's 200th year |
| **One man and his tens** (Khaganate): units follow a man, not a state | **Succession.** Every khaganate has split at a khagan's death; the Felt Keepers name the heir; a rival wing-khan has bought neutrality | a rival's claim, a pre-emptive strike, a hidden heir whose blood is the secret |
| **The bought soldier** (Kethra, Navesht): boys bought, raised, paid by village revenue | **They cannot breed themselves**; sons born free may not command the bought; a month's pay late is a revolt; the king's sons can never be king | a yearly need for captives, an election with knives, a marriage offered to the enemy |
| **The temple ledger** (Vahra): rank by debts of past lives | **The two ledgers** (public and true); no horses of its own | priesthood and throne fall together if proved |
| **The temple-mountain** (Pravith): the king is the world; tanks keep the rains | **The tank silts**, survey hidden; tributaries leave for the strongest circle | a famine blamed on the king, a vassal's defection |
| **The toll** (Suraeng): every ship pays | A secret bribe from the emperor to keep silk off the sea; a rising rival fleet | a double dealing found out, a rival's war |
| **The households** (Menaro): forty warrior houses under an elected marshal | **Honour**: a lord who charges alone; an election; a plan to make the office hereditary by marriage | a feud that becomes war when a neighbour notices |
| **The age-sets and the wall** (Ndorima): regiments by initiation | The gold reefs fell by a third; graves robbed to meet the coast's demand | a want for the coast's ports to pay for it |
| **The drums** (lake kingdoms): the drum is the kingdom | The chief drum is a copy; four men know | whoever holds the true drum can claim the realm |
| **The women's houses, the elected Speaker, the sold silence** (lesser realms) | each its own | each its own |

**The pattern behind them.** A great state fails where its *pay*, its *succession* or its *shared belief* is faked or
strained, and the people who profit from the fake keep it hidden. That is exactly the owner's rule: no coup, rising or
war without a reason that someone could name.

## 3 · Reasons for war and quarrel: a catalogue

Mapped to `war.md` §11.2 kinds. Each row is a real Codex case, so a builder sees what a reason looks like when it has a
source and a person behind it. (Use the shape, not the Far Lands name.)

| Kind (§11.2) | Case | The reason, in the Codex's own logic |
|---|---|---|
| **Succession and claim** | The khagan's last winter (Q1) | Tegur is 73, has not ridden since autumn; Jagdet has waited 43 years, bought a neutral clan and courted a march; the Keepers have buried the choice of Ulgai in a sealed felt. If he dies first, Jagdet claims the felt. |
| **Claim** | Hesh's emperor and his "mad" brother (EVT-2065) | Bargai had elder brother Nikhon declared mad and took the crown; Nikhon is sane, held in a tower, has a son of 14. A claimant in hand is a reason for anyone who hates Bargai. |
| **Greed and need** | Spring on the Sau (Q2) | Hesh arms to cross; a general will open a gate for a crown; a king will leave a gorge open; a duke will cut his own dykes when the other crosses. Each believes his is the only secret. |
| **Fear** | The circle of Pravith (Q6) | The tributaries smell the leaking tank, so they drift to the next circle; its king claims by his mother and wins them one by one. |
| **Injury** | The Strait (Q4) | A planned seizure in 1106 with a bought harbour, a bought neutral and a harbour changing sides; a scout order sells each side the other's plans. |
| **Home** (rising) | The Black Lamp (Q3) | A failed examinee of 1078 who wrote a true answer burns census rolls; fed by smugglers, armed by a hill confederacy, nursed by an abbey. Three armies beaten. A pardon could split him. |
| **Home** (refusal) | EVT-2068 | A general refused an order to march on the rising and took a commandery: a refusal needs a grievance, a command, and an opening. |
| **Duty** | Horses for the Gulf (Q5) | Two kingdoms fight most dry seasons over a horse road; a third trades horses for riders; a fourth buys riders to fight its own temples. |
| **Injury** (hidden) | The basins' boys (Q8) | A purse bought 300 boys taken from the other wing's clans; if the khaganate learns it, a trade becomes a war. |
| **The moment** | Sunrise election (Q7) | A nine-year term ends in 1106; one house means to make it hereditary; the others, and the march lord, mean to stop him. |
| **Greed** | The silk that leaks (Q10) | The law broken in three places; the office that hid it from three emperors; when it comes out someone must be punished and someone wants the price kept. |
| **Insult and pride** | Dyke-breaking and calendars | The Hesh broke its dykes five times since 640 BR, "each time a dynasty fell within thirty years": a belief that makes a flood a political fact. |
| **Duty (faith)** | The census burned, the drum stolen | The faith's object becomes the casus. |

**Causal chains the Codex writes out** (use as worked examples for R1 audits):

1. *Debased coin → arrears → mutiny → a general's refusal → a breakaway commandery* (1081 to 1098).
2. *A khagan's death → two wings → silk road shut twenty years → prices triple in the far west* (842 to 862).
3. *A plague spreads along a trade road to the far end* (909 to 915): reasons travel the trade lanes.
4. *A hired garrison that will not march home founds its own town* (1062): a reason for new realms.

## 4 · Hidden facts as levers (the Codex's "Secret" row)

118 realm secrets, 55 faith secrets, 10 mysteries. Their useful kinds, each a thing a decider acts on when he learns it:

- **A bloodline fact** (heir is a grandson, a quarter foreign; a claimed descent is false by bronze tablets).
- **A bookkeeping fact** (three notes written for two recorded; two ledgers; silting tanks; falling gold).
- **A bought man** (a heir in a rival's pay; a younger brother a rebel captain; a king's vassal in secret).
- **A false object** (a copy of the chief drum).
- **A deal** (a yearly bribe, a tithe, a march lord backing a house for a promise).

The game's `secrets` list (71) and the belief-versus-truth system (`src/world/26-belief-versus-truth`) already carry
"accepted story / truth / who knows / proof". The Codex rows fit that shape unchanged.

## 5 · Dates and the weight of time

The Codex ties durations to danger: dynasties under 120 years are firm; the oldest (455 years) are the most brittle.
Wars last "most dry seasons" when what is fought for is a road, and stop for the monsoon (planting and rice calendars
forbid war in some seasons). For §11 and the calendar: *a war can be barred by a season, a harvest or a rite*, and that
is a reason, not a rule.
