# 99995-v2-nobility: every realm's court, from the counts up and down

**Aspect:** the owner's v2 complaint 13, "too few nobles" (`docs/v2/V2_SPEC.md`), integration step 6 · **Kind:** system
(two core files, no patches, no events) on Ardan v2's engine (`A.V2.nobility`) and the family lane (`A.V2F`).

## What it does

**A pyramid per realm** (`a-court.core.js`). At world start each realm's counties are the provinces it owns in
`A.S.prov` (never the map's geometry: the map lane is replacing it). `A.V2.nobility.buildHierarchy` cuts them into
titles: at most 3 dukes (24 counties or more: 3, 14: 2, 7: 1), at most 6 marquises on the borders (2 or 3 a duke),
one count a county, then 2-3 viscounts a count, 2-4 barons a viscount and 3-6 lords a baron. A realm of 20-22 counties
has 850-1,100 nobles; a 30-county realm about 1,300.

| Tier | Stored? | Rung (`GD.NOBLE_RUNGS`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| King, or the realm's ruler | the release's ruler; the crown holds its capital county | the crown |
| Duke, marquis | a person in `A.S.people`, heading a house in `A.S.houses` | Duke 8, Marquis 7 (`p.noble.court`) |
| Count | the same | Count 6, holding the county |
| Viscount, baron, lord | generated by `lowerNoble()` until met | Viscount 5, Baron 4, Lord on Knight's rung 2 (`p.noble.fief`) |

- **The release's great houses keep their land.** A great house of ducal, marcher or comital rank (N01-N03) seated in a
  county holds it: v2 starts a march or a duchy at its seat (`opts.dukeAt` / `opts.marchAt`, an engine change), and
  otherwise it is that county's count. Its rung follows the title it holds (`h.v2rung` keeps the old one). A lesser
  great house (N04-N07) seated in a county takes the first viscounty, barony or manor of its rank under that county's
  count. Every other title gets a new house (arms, levy from the county's people, 9996's contract on its first New
  Year) and a head made by the game's `A.genNoble`, with 0-3 children (1-3 for a duke or marquis) so the line can pass.
- **One holder per title.** The title's key rides on `p.noble.court`; titles move only in the family lane's
  `A.V2F.inherit` (its `giveLordship` copies `p.noble`). When an heir already had a rung of their own the key is added
  to theirs (`p.noble.more`). `A.V2N.holderOf(t)` follows the first holder's line; a county held with a duchy or march
  has no holder of its own.
- **Lower nobles on demand.** `A.V2N.lower(rid, county, rank, i)` is deterministic from the seed, the realm, the county,
  the rank and the index (`lowerNoble()` for the sex and the fief's name; the game's name makers for the realm's
  culture, run with the game's stream and 97's "names lately used" set aside). Nothing is stored until someone meets
  one: `A.V2N.meet(...)` makes the person once and records the slot in `A.S.court.met`, so meeting them again is the
  same person. A new barony or viscounty with no county of its own (994's enfeoffment, 97's patent, a ruler's grant,
  now that every county has its count) takes the first free slot of its rank under the count of the grantee's county
  (`A.V2N.grantFief`, from 994's `CAR.enfeoff` and each month): "Baron of Marlford".
- **Old saves** without a court build one on their first month, from the seed. A realm born later (a revolt, a split)
  keeps the counts its counties had; it gets no court of its own.

**What you see** (`b-view.core.js`). The court view (and the Realm view, for a ruler) has *The great lords of
<realm>*: how many nobles hold land, tier by tier, in words and numbers (no percentages); what the crown holds itself
and which counts answer to it; each duke with the counts and marches under him; the marches that answer to the crown;
and your own county, with its count and its viscounts by name (call on one to meet them) and its barons and lords
counted. A county that has since been taken by another realm says so. One small card a row (90-fold would fold a tall
card). All of it is pure.

## Small edits elsewhere

- `src/v2/nobility.js` (`node tools/build-v2.mjs`): `buildHierarchy(realm, map, seed, opts)` takes preferred seats for
  marches and duchies, and the marches leave four counties a duchy, so a realm always gets the dukes `dukesFor` gives
  (before, six marches of 2-4 counties could eat a 21-county realm and leave one duke under six marquises). Tests in
  `tests/v2/world.test.js`.
- `99994-v2-family/c-succession.core.js`: with a court, only a house of title rank (N01-N03) holds its seat's county,
  in `F.freeHolding` and `F.audit`; and a court count whose county becomes a realm's capital keeps it (the audit no
  longer calls that two holders).
- `994-careers/careers.core.js`: `CAR.enfeoff` gives a barony under a count when no county is free.

## Determinism

The pyramid draws from v2's `makeRng` seeded from the game's seed; the new people are made by the game's own
generators inside `A.V2F.sandbox`, so the game's stream stands where the release left it after world start. What moves:
`A.S.nextId` (about 550 more people at start: ~300 holders and their children), about 230 new houses, and the yearly
draws for them (house opinion drift, ageing, deaths), so a life's later draws differ from the family lane's build.
