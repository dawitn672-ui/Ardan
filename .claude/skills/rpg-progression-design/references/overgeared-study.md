# Overgeared: a study of its progression (saved for order 97)

The owner's order 97 asks that titles be reworked "after a study of *Overgeared*'s titles" and that stats "say exactly
what each point gives ... with *Overgeared* in mind; the study saved in the knowledge base". This is that study.

*Overgeared* (오버기어드) is a Korean web novel by Park Saenal (2016 on), adapted as a webtoon. Its world is **Satisfy**,
a virtual-reality game of over two billion players run by a supercomputer. The hero, Grid (Shin Youngwoo), gets the
legendary class **Pagma's Successor** (first translated **Pagma's Descendant**: 파그마의 후예). The title of the novel is Korean
gamer slang for a player who leans on his gear rather than his skill.

**How the facts were checked.** Read from the Overgeared Fandom wiki's page sources (MediaWiki API, 2026-11-27), whose
pages cite the novel's chapters (the chapter numbers below are the wiki's). The wiki itself marks many pages "Needs More
Details" or "Needs Verification", and the author changed some numbers mid-story. **Marks:** *(ch N)* is the chapter the
wiki cites; **[unsure]** means the wiki flags it, contradicts itself, or I could only infer it. Nothing here comes from
memory alone.

---

## 1. Levels and experience

- **10 free stat points every level** ("Generally, 10 free stat points are given for gaining every level", *Savior of the
  World* page, ch 578). Grid's sheets agree: 40 points at level 1, 60 at 3, 110 at 8.
- **A level 1 character's base stats:** 6 strength, 7 stamina, 4 agility, 5 intelligence (*Stats*). **[unsure]** The
  wiki tags this page "Needs Verification".
- **Steep experience curve.** From Grid's status windows (*Grid/Levels*), the experience to the next level was 100 at
  level 1, 500 at 3, 1,400 at 8, 16,700 at 21 and 238,000 at 45. That is about ×2,400 between levels 1 and 45.
- **Advancements every 100 levels** (*Classes*): levels 100-199 are the First Advancement, 200-299 the Second, and so on.
  Each one gives the class more skills or unique stats. The **Third** gives normal classes a quest with several class-change
  options. The **Fourth** opens the *Enlightenment* system, which lets a player personalise his class until he can beat
  named NPCs.
- **Stats awakening** (ch 136): "the effect of the stats increase every 100 levels" (§2). The 100-level step changes
  what every point already placed is worth, not only how many points there are.
- **A legendary class resets the level to 1** on the class change (*Legends*, ch 287, 853, 914).
- **Death costs experience** (players under level 10 lose none, *Satisfy*). Two deaths in 24 hours lock a player out for
  12 hours. Those are game-service rules with no world reason.

## 2. Stats: what a point gives

Satisfy splits stats into the **four basic stats**, which take free points (strength, stamina, agility, intelligence), and
**special stats**, which "can't be distributed to". Special stats are opened by a class, a title or a deed and raised by
doing. A title's "all stats +X" lifts only some of the special stats (*Grid/Titles*, notes).

### Basic stats, per point, by awakening

| Stat | Before level 100 | 1st (100+) | 2nd (200+) | 3rd (300+) | 4th (400+) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strength** | 0.1 attack, 3 health, +20 weight limit | 0.2 attack, 5 health, +30 weight | 0.3 attack, 5 health, +40 weight | 0.7 attack, 7 health, +50 weight | 0.8 attack, 9 health |
| **Stamina** | not given | 0.2 defense | 20 health | 25 health, 0.9 defense | 30 health, 1.2 defense |
| **Intelligence** | not given | 0.5 magic power | 2 magic power, 6 mana | 1.2 magic power, 6 mana | not given |

Sources: *Strength* (ch 136, 292, 295, 849, 959, 1070), *Stamina (Stat)* (ch 136, 292, 295, 1070), *Intelligence* (ch 136,
176, 181, 311, 396).

**[unsure]** The author rewrote the formulas around chapter 300. Strength's 3rd awakening was 0.6 attack in chapters 295
and 849 and 0.7 in 959. The wiki calls the intelligence numbers careless ("the author is not paying attention"), and
stamina's 2nd and 3rd steps were "rephrased" by the wiki.

- **Agility:** every 1,000 points gives +0.1 attack speed, and at 3,000 the top running speed rises by 30. A player's usual
  top speed is 100 metres in five seconds (ch 684, 1092).
- **Intelligence** also gates spells. A spell does not cast if the intelligence is too low to understand its formula (ch
  396). Among third-advancement players, more than 1,000 intelligence is "extremely rare" unless they are magicians or scholars.
- **Dexterity:** a production class's stat. It rises by making items and is "the easiest stat to raise" for a producer (ch 928).
- **A race contract can multiply a stat's coefficient.** Grid's Twilight Orc contract multiplied stamina by 1.8, so a
  point gave 54 health instead of 30 and 2.1 defense instead of 1.2 (ch 1100).

### Special stats (no free points; raised by doing or given)

| Stat | What it does | How it rises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persistence | +20 weight limit a point (calculated); you tire later; you stay fed longer; every 10 points give +1 Indomitable | by persisting at hard work (chopping 1,000 logs) |
| Indomitable | a chance to cancel part of a blow, higher at lower health; seen as a "trash stat" for its rarity: Grid never saw it fire at nearly 900 | from Persistence; legendary classes |
| Dignity | "can't be pushed down"; a rare chance that the enemy submits; at 850 "nobles gulp"; past 1,500, low and mid players freeze as if stunned | titles (First Duke +600); legendary classes |
| Insight | read a target's abilities, stats and skills; predict risks; "a definite upper limit" | by observing; Commander classes from the start; long management of a guild or estate |
| Composure | resist and recover from abnormal states; 400 is enough to regain calm at once | made items' bonuses (easier for producers) |
| Courage | +0.1 attack and defense a point; from 1,200 points, +0.2 | opened by the Apostle of Justice title |
| Fighting Spirit | the lower the health, the greater the attack | the Competitor title |
| Charm, Leadership, Killing Intent, Persuasion, Faith, Divine Power, Nobility, Madness, Willpower, Deity ... | named by class or deed (an assassin's killing intent, an orator's persuasion, a berserker's madness) | class, title, quest; often only mentioned **[unsure]** |

Sources: the wiki's pages of the same names and *Stats/Minimal Information*.

**What this teaches:** a point's meaning is **stated in the game's own units** (attack, health, weight, mana). It **grows
at each 100-level step**, so the walls reward points already placed. The **character stats** (dignity, insight, composure,
courage) are **earned by living a certain way**, not bought with points.

## 3. Classes

- **Over 120,000 classes** exist in Satisfy (*Satisfy*). Three types: combat, production and other non-combat (*Classes*).
- **Ratings:** normal; then the hidden classes, rated rare, epic, unique and (very limited) legendary; and later myth. "Every
  class with a rare or higher rating is a hidden class" (*Rating*). Some hidden classes are **growth-type** and can rise in
  rating, even to legendary or myth, by meeting conditions.
- **Normal classes "possess the greatest potential per advancement"** (*Classes*). By the Third Advancement the gap to a
  rare hidden class had narrowed so far that rare classes stopped selling for fortunes (ch 362). The Nine Legendary
  classes were meant to lead "until the normal classes catch up with them ... up to the fourth" (ch 182).
- **Legendary classes:** "a total of nine legendary classes based on the nine previous legends" (*Legends*, ch 182). The
  conditions "cannot be achieved by deliberately targeting the traces left behind", but one failure does not close the
  door (ch 849).
  - **One holder at a time.** The Sword Saint passed from Biban (second) to Muller (third) to Kraugel (*Sword Saint*). The
    class quest ends with an SS-rated quest, "Beat a Legend", a one-to-one duel with a legend.
  - **New legendary classes can be pioneered** by deeds (Piaro; Mercedes became a **Legendary Knight** by proving her
    loyalty and breaking her limits against a weakened great demon, ch 807).
  - "A legendary class is not an all-rounder" (ch 1279). Pagma's Successor was weaker in combat than the other former
    legends' classes (ch 1402).
- **What a legendary class gives** (*Pagma's Successor*, *Sword Saint*, *Legendary Knight*, *One who Became a Legend*):
  - the title **One who Became a Legend**: resistance to all abnormal states except physical ones, five seconds of
    immortality at a fatal blow (24-hour cooldown), and easy regard from NPCs;
  - **special stats** opened (composure, indomitable, dignity, insight);
  - **rule-breaking class lines**: Pagma's Successor can wear any item, with a penalty by rating (rare −20%, epic −30%,
    legendary −55% of the base abilities, and only half the item's effects). The Sword Saint brings out swords' hidden
    functions. The Legendary Knight can wear all arms except magic ones;
  - **a creation skill**: the Sword Saint creates new sword techniques, the Legendary Knight creates knights and codes. The
    number of creations grows with the class's mastery skill level. "There is usually at least one creation skill for
    every legend" (*Legends*);
  - **drawbacks**: Pagma's Successor had no auto-production, gained no level from crafting, had no combat mastery, and
    his sword dances need footwork and a spoken name.
- **Myth classes:** above legendary. They need "high status and Deity". One received doubles health, mana and sword energy
  (*Myths*, ch 1466).

## 4. Titles

- **How they are earned:** "obtained after a player has made a direct connection to Satisfy's setting or stories". To
  remove them "would collapse" the setting (ch 600). High rankers average **12 titles**; those who overcame every trial
  hold **30 or more**, most of them superb (ch 1062).
- **Kinds seen** (examples from *Grid/Titles*, *Titles/Minimal Information* and the title pages):

| Kind | Example | Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slayer of a kind | Knight Slayer | kill a knight; "the easiest title", a ranker's mark | Stamina +100, Strength +30 |
| First to do X (once ever) | First Unique Item Maker; Only Legendary Item Maker | make the first unique / legendary item | Dexterity +200; +350 |
| Quest and deed | Apostle of Justice | rescue a prisoner (a quest) | All stats +10; **opens the Courage stat**; **generates a skill**; below 10% health all stats +30% |
| Saviour of a realm | Kingdom's Hero | six who stopped a golem invasion | All stats +120; the highest reputation in that kingdom |
| Rank and office | First Duke | the first duke | Dignity +600, Charm +800 |
| World raid | Savior of the World (legendary) | everyone in the Belial raid | All stats +200 (+300 when enhanced by the Berith raid); a three-hour buff when a minstrel sings your saga |
| Touching the beyond | Man who has Touched Hell; Dipped your Feet into the World of the Gods | going there | Health +3,000; +10,000 |
| Share boosts | Duke of Virtue; Duke of Fire | his divinity defined | Stamina +35% and a skill; Willpower +20% and a skill |
| Status of a legend | One who Became a Legend | a legendary class | §3 |
| Killing a myth | Myth Usurper | kill a being of a myth | may give Deity |
| A being's own | Transcendent Dragon | a dragon using skills above its level | a temporary stat boost |
| Curse | Goddess' Curse | attacking a church unprovoked | opens the *Unlucky* stat |

- **Titles grow and change.** Glimpsed the Myths became First Myth. Qualification of a Blood King became Blood King
  Candidate, then Blood King. One who Became a Legend was replaced when he became a myth. Savior of the World was
  *enhanced* by a second raid.
- **Titles can be lost or sealed:** Earl's Son-in-law was lost, Pioneer was lost after an 18th epic, and Watched by the
  Gods was permanently sealed (*Grid/Titles*).
- **Rarity of titles:** the wiki calls Savior of the World "a legendary title" (ch 673). Most titles carry no stated
  rating. **[unsure]** There may be no general title rarity system in the novel. Rarity there is mostly a property of
  classes, items and skills.
- **Size:** Grid's 54 titles sum to all stats +430, Dexterity +550, Dignity +600, Charm +800 and Health +13,000, plus share
  boosts (*Grid/Titles*). Against 10 points a level, **one great title is worth tens of levels** ("a player with 10 stats
  would have to gain 200 levels" to match Savior of the World's +200, ch 578).
- **How a title is announced:** system messages, and for world events world-wide notices. A player is told of a title when
  it comes. Before that, only rumour and lore speak of the conditions (Courage "is heard about in rumors", ch 33).

## 5. Skills

- **Skill levels** usually run 1 to 10. Some have **stages** (beginner, intermediate, advanced, master), each 1 to 10. A
  production class's stage sets **the best rating it can make**: normal at beginner, rare at intermediate, epic at
  advanced, unique at master (*Skills*, ch 10).
- **Mastery passives** grow only by use: Sword Mastery raises attack power and attack speed with a sword, in beginner,
  intermediate and advanced stages. The **Knight** class can learn every mastery, but "the experience of mastery skills
  increased only when using a weapon suitable for that mastery". Most knights therefore keep to one weapon (ch 882).
- **Rarity of skills:** "hidden skills" are rare or better (ch 362). They come from special quests, achievements and titles.
  - **Rare skills often deal fixed damage**, the strongest at first, then obsolete as health grows (ch 104, 172).
  - **Legendary skills scale with the user**: "the stronger the user got, the stronger the skill" (ch 104).
- **Area skills:** 50-70% of attack at the first advancement, 70-90% above level 200, and over 100% for a legendary one (ch 104).
- **Skill trees** branch at every 100 levels. A lower-rated tree can be improved by fusing outside skills into it to graft
  a new branch. "If one specialises enough, they can be as strong as Legendary classes" (ch 1557).
- **A legendary skill, in numbers** (*Pagma's Sword Dance*, ch 78, 229, 914). At level 1, passive: +20% physical attack,
  +10% critical. Active: five sword techniques, 20 mana, and the passive is lost while active. Ten seconds to switch back.
  At level 2: +30% attack, +20% critical, +10% critical damage, seven techniques. The drawbacks are long cast times and
  footwork.
- **Merging and raising:**
  - **Skill Synthesis** turns three least-used active skills (not unique ones) into one new skill. Its uses grow every three
    levels of Transcendence (ch 1204-1205).
  - **Skill Enhancement** strengthens one class skill (a god's reward, ch 876).
  - A **Skill Level Point** raises a skill's level (ch 1165).
  - **Evolution by understanding:** taking a myth weapon fully apart evolved Grid's craftsmanship skill and raised every
    production skill by one (ch 271).
- **Mental worlds** are special skills that embody the user's inner mind, enhanced by sacrificing skills (ch 1139, 1479).

## 6. Items, ratings and sets

- **One rating ladder** for classes, items and user skills, in words: **Normal, Rare, Epic, Unique, Legendary, Myth**,
  with colours (white, blue, purple, red, gold). Quests and NPC talents use **letters F to SSSS**. Some letters match
  words: an S-grade item was Unique, an SS-grade skill Legendary (*Rating*). Special ratings sit beside them: **Only One**
  (the first to do what no legend or myth did), **Ancient**, **Secret**, **growth type**, and the sub-rating
  **(Transcendent)**. **[unsure]** A **Divine** rating above Myth is mentioned once.
- **Value climbs steeply with rating** (*Rating*). Rare equipment is worth about 3× normal and epic about 7× (ch 21). Epic
  sells for over 400,000 gold (ch 157), unique for tens to hundreds of thousands, legendary for at least two million.
  Myth items are owned by "more than a two digit number of people" **[unsure: translation]**. Legendary items became
  common for top players by the fourth and fifth years (Asuka held 45). The world inflates over time.
- **Unique is "the highest class rating for those who are not a legend"** (*Rating*).
- **Use conditions:** most items ask a level, stats, a skill or a class. Only legendary classes ignore them (*Equipment*).
- **Enhancement** +0 to +10. Each + adds 5% of base stats (7% from +6). Up to +5 almost always succeeds; from +6 it rarely
  does. A failure costs −3 (ordinary stone), −1 (blessed stone) or nothing (blessed scroll). The first to reach +10 gets
  a title (*Item Enhancement*). Pure gambling.
- **Sets:** the Holy Light Set's three pieces together give +500 defense and +6,000 health (*Holy Light Set*).
- **Growth-type items** gain experience in use and rise in rating. Either a soul's skills bloom with each rating, or an
  option fitted to the user is added each time (*Growth Type Items*).
- **Item Understanding** (Pagma's Successor): by appraising, using, repairing and taking apart an item he understands it.
  At 100% he can make it, and understanding lowers the penalty for wearing what he does not meet (*Item Understanding*).

## 7. Above levels: legends, transcendence, myths

- **Legend is a status made of feats** that people keep telling. It **fades as fame fades**: "if the fame is weak ... the
  status of a legend falls" (ch 1000). The legends of the previous generation were at least level 600 in their prime
  (ch 1183). A legend able to threaten a great demon comes "normally one person per era" (ch 573).
- **Transcendence** is a second axis ("status"). It is raised by feats: entering a dragon's lair, beating far stronger
  foes, reaching great skill, breaking one's race's limits (ch 1070, 1272). Each step gives senses, resistances, skills and
  long life (*Transcendence*).
- **Myth** is above legend. It is made by others' faith, by an "accumulation of lore" (a monster becomes a mythical
  creature), or by remarkable feats (*Myths*).
- **Epics:** rare story events, as rare as making a myth item. They raise transcendence and Deity, and during one the
  story's praise buffs and debuffs those involved (*Epic*).

## 8. What fits Ardan's rule 18 ("nothing happens without reason"), and what does not

| Overgeared's choice | Fit | Ardan's form of it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A stat point stated in units (attack, health, weight) | **fits** | a stat's sheet note in Ardan's own units (blood, carry in pounds, a step of a fight's worth), never a percentage |
| A point's worth rising at each 100-level step (awakening) | **fits**, with a reason | the wall is a breakthrough of the body (order 95); the coefficient rises at each wall crossed, recalibrating the power ladder |
| Special stats opened and raised by living (persistence, insight, composure, dignity) | **fits well** | traits of conduct a person earns by deeds and posts (a steward's insight, a knight's composure), never with points |
| 10 points a level, stats in the thousands | **does not fit** | Ardan's body has a ceiling (potential × 1.02) and room by age; take the shape, not the size |
| Normal classes catch up by later advancements | **fits** | keeps a commoner's road worth walking; the base line's late steps are its strength |
| Legendary class: one holder, inherited by a qualified successor or won by beating the holder | **fits** | `A.S.lvc.u` census (branch o98-rarity); a succession needs a reason (a pupil, a duel, a deed) |
| Legendary class resets the level to 1 | **does not fit** | Ardan's level is the power ladder's (one scale): a class never moves the level |
| Legend status fading with fame | **fits** | renown (`src/people/99999-v2-renown`) already fades; a title's social weight may follow it |
| Titles from story deeds with hidden conditions; first-to titles | **fits** | the deed ledger (`A.deedDone`), `once` and `since` titles |
| Rumours about titles; world-wide notices | **does not fit** | order 92: no hints anywhere; a title is told to its holder when it comes, in the world's words |
| "All stats +300" from one raid | **does not fit** | a snowball with no body behind it; Ardan's caps (+10 a stat from all sources, the budget, the boost pool) |
| A title that opens a stat or gives a skill | **fits** | the title's gift is a new thing to do (order 92's rule for classes), within the budget |
| Conditional title passives (below 10% health, all stats +30%) | **fits** | the skills' `trigger` effects (near_death, outnumbered) at their price |
| Mastery grows only with the weapon used | **fits** (order 88) | `A.gainXp` by use, the knight who keeps to one weapon |
| Rare skills of fixed damage that age badly; legendary skills that scale | **fits** | rarity buys scaling and new verbs, not one big number |
| A smith's skill stage caps the rating he can make | **fits** | a made thing's rarity read from the maker's level and the material, never rolled |
| Enhancement by stones: +1 or −3 by luck | **does not fit** as written | a smith's reworking, with risk from his skill and the metal's flaws, not a die |
| Myth by players' faith; Deity; gods | **does not fit** (order 83: mostly no gods) | Ardan's mythical rank is for the few legends' own sets (order 97), not a faith score |
| Five seconds of immortality, immunity to all states | **only with a cause** | "a legend doesn't yield" read as will and composure firing under a trigger, priced and capped |
| Over 120,000 classes | **does not fit** | order 97: as many as are worth having, never padding |
