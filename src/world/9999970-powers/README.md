# 9999970-powers: one power scale for every being (powers wave 1)

**Aspect:** the owner's order of 2026-11-25 ("make the powerscaling of this world much larger"); master prompt section 17
(monsters, legendary beings) · **Kind:** system, four readable core files, one event pack, three data files. Design and
numbers: `docs/design/powers.md`.

## Files

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `a-power.core.js` | `A.POWER`: the level (`level`, `might`, `being`, `band`), the profile matchup (`compare`), titles and their buffs, a duel fighter for any species (`fighter`), the ranked great beings (`great`). Pure |
| `b-wilds.core.js` | `A.onMonth`: the legends' seasons and the summit's yearly sleep or waking (A.WHY), raids and the crowns' answers, the wild monsters' year (feed, fight, rise, take a name), the player's encounter roll, the hunt (`A.POWER.hunt`), the year's tale and the Sword Saint's childhood story |
| `c-meet.core.js` | The cards' odds and outcomes: flee, calm, fight (duel when close, settled at once when far), speak (A.WHY), and meeting a great being |
| `d-view.core.js` | `UI.powersPage`: the codex's Power & scale page gains the level scale, the great beings the player has heard of, the beasts he has met (click notes, no percentages) |
| `powers.events.js` | `pw_encounter`, `pw_great` (queued only) |
| `data/power.json` | the scale, ranks, odds constants, capabilities, intelligence classes, the race masters |
| `data/bestiary.json` | 270 kinds, generated by `tools/powers/gen-bestiary.mjs` (`--check` says whether it is current) |
| `data/legends.json` | 16 legends with full character sheets |

## Release edits (guarded, commented)

- `A.fighterBeast` (06): a species not in `A.BEASTS` gets its fighter from `A.POWER.fighter`.
- `A.startHunt` (07): asks `A.POWER.hunt` first.
- `A.eventView` (18a): a choice may carry `odds(pc, ctx)` (4-96), for a fight the power scale settles.
- The codex's power page (23f): appends `UI.powersPage`.

No `A.addStage`; no bare dice for a decision (legends, crowns, named monsters and speaking beasts choose through A.WHY).
