# Retired: 63-miniature-portraits (2026-11-13, C8b)

**What it was:** the woodcut "painted miniature" portraits (`miniatures.core.js`, 107 KB): an ink line and a two-tone
wash on a gold or lapis ground, with a table of what each culture wears. It wrapped `PORTRAIT.svg` under
93-anime-portraits and was what the game drew when the anime setting was off.

**Why retired:** as 93-anime-portraits: the figure library (`src/ui/93-figures`, D-035) draws everyone now, and D-015
and D-020 settled that the two older renderers retire.

**Carried over:** the culture table (`CULT`: headwear for men and women, a people's dye), as
`src/ui/93-figures/figures.a-peoples.core.js`. The menu setting `UI.set.miniatures` is gone.

**Last commit that built it:** `f574229`.
