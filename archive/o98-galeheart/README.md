# Galeheart, the old storm ancient (retired by order 98, D-198)

Order 97 made Vaskareth the storm ancient: one dragon, far above the inferno and water ancients. Galeheart, the
Ancient Storm Dragon of D-193 (level 780, "it", the King's Thunder), was a second storm lord beside her, so it was folded
into her: its post (keeping the roads of the sky and the dragons to their own skies) and its grudge against Stormwing
are hers now. The bestiary's `ancient-storm-dragon` points at her sheet (`legend: "vaskareth"`).

- `galeheart.json`: its profile as it stood in `data/figures/ancient-dragons.json`.
- `the-kings-thunder.json`: its title from `data/titles/figures.json` (no other holder).

The last commit that had them in data: 006705b4 (branch o98-demons).
