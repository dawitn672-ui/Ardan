# Changelog

What changed for the player, in plain words, one section per version (newest first). The technical record is
`docs/HISTORY.md`; the list of versions and their commits is `docs/VERSIONS.md`.

## Unreleased

## v2.1.0-alpha.3 (2026-11-13 02:40 ET) · The game's own code made readable

- Nothing changes in play: the game is now built from readable source instead of the packed release file, proven to
  run exactly the same. Every later fix and feature is easier and safer because of it.

## v2.1.0-alpha.2 (2026-11-13 01:35 ET) · Foundations: lighter steps, click-to-open, Next month or Next year

- Descriptions open when you click or tap, never on hover; long explanations sit behind a small ⓘ. An activity
  shows what it is, what it costs and how it may go before you commit, and its result is never cut short.
- Next month is much lighter: the page redraws once per press, the story log updates in place, and a long life no
  longer gets slower. Starting a life shows a progress line.
- Fixes: the duel's hint about your foe's move is right; the Adapting bar uses words; a passed-over heir is told why;
  phone buttons and person rows are the right size; no two-year-old sits on a royal council.
- **Next month or Next year.** Two buttons sit in fixed places, on a phone and on a desktop, and neither ever turns into
  a stop button. A year runs month by month and stops early only for something that matters (a real decision, a call
  to war, an illness, a birth or death at home, coming of age), saying why; everyday chatter waits for the end of the
  step. Childhood moves by the year.
- **No more initiative.** Do as much as you like; do too much and you grow weary, then hurt.
- A phone's clock now shows your age ("Age 9 · March 1105").
- Every screen draws faster again (a slowdown that came with Next year is gone).

## v2.1.0-alpha.1 (2026-11-12 11:20 ET) · Round 2 begins

- The game no longer slows down after you have seen a hint or lived on as an heir: a month late in a long life
  costs about half what it did.
- A court bug that could leave two lords on one seat for the rest of a game is fixed.
- "Would teach you f" and "1 armies in the field" now read properly.

- Nothing changes in play yet: the game's files were reorganised so the work ahead (lag, ranks, click-to-open
  descriptions, a cleaner screen, real households, real-world names, distinct faces, a detailed map, real military
  branches and deeper war) has a clear home.

## v2.0.0-beta.1 (Nov 6)

- Cards tell you what is at stake, including when a failure can kill you.
- War by rank: a soldier hears rough numbers and rumours, a general sees exact ones; wars you are not in stay rumour.
- Kingdoms have real names; families and clans make sense; bodies are judged against people their own age.
- Words instead of 0–100 numbers; map, layout and phone fixes; wars come at a sensible pace; advisors no longer lose
  their turn on scenes you never see.

## v2.0.0-alpha.1 to alpha.10 (Nov 1–5)

- One generated continent replaces the two maps. One "Next month" button; months bring cards one at a time and quiet
  months a short note. Bodies in real units (kilograms, metres a second). Three generations of family, and house law
  for succession. Courts from dukes down to lords. Relations between realms as named reasons. You see only your own
  war, at your rank. Saves carry all of it.

## v1.x (Oct 12 – Nov 1)

- The shipped release, then rounds of fixes and features on top of it: the chronicle, honest odds, sieges, named
  squads, schemes, story arcs, realm customs, the redesign (Woodblock & Light), journeys, the atlas, chapters, share
  cards, the polity, companies, stakes on every choice and advisors.
