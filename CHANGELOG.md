# Changelog

What changed for the player, in plain words, one section per version (newest first). The technical record is
`docs/HISTORY.md`; the list of versions and their commits is `docs/VERSIONS.md`.

## Unreleased

- **Plain names, a flavour for every realm.** People are named from one large pool of ordinary English names, and each realm leans
  its own way (Aldmark English, Northreach Norse, Greyhaven German, Solenne French, Varenna Italian), while the far and
  non-human peoples keep names of their own. Ports and border towns hold people from the realm across the border, no town has
  two of one name, and brothers, sisters and cousins no longer share a name.
- **Four realms are named for what they are:** the Khanate of Ulun, the League of Brannoch, the Free Port of Corvasse and the
  Ughrak Clans. Fiefs are named in their own people's language.
- **You are born knowing your household, not the whole clan.** A child knows its parents, its brothers and sisters, and a
  grandparent who lives under the same roof; a noble child also has a nurse, a maid, a guard knight and, from six, a tutor.
  Uncles, aunts and cousins come to a feast or a burial and you meet them then, and every person you know says how and
  where you met. A scene never invents a sister you do not have.
- **The village has faces.** Your parish has its priest, reeve, miller, smith and neighbours, the same people every time,
  and a child of the village plays with the village's children. Children are born nine months after they are conceived,
  in any month of the year.

## v2.1.0-alpha.6 (2026-11-15 04:20 ET) · Screens that fit the life, a faster phone start, one holder for every command

- **Screens know who you are.** A newborn is not offered a physician or told how he looks; a child of nine has no crimes, no
  plots, no Arms page and no quarrel to pick with his father; a squire is not told to find work or to rob travellers; a
  Chancellor is not offered the army or the dice; a captain in garrison has no War Room. What a life can never do is not
  listed at all, instead of a button that says "Not in your position".
- **One list of what to do next.** What now, your ambition, the chapter's aim and what is coming up (a loan due, a custom,
  a feast) are one list in one order, five lines at most, instead of four cards that said the same thing.

- **A faster start, on a phone most of all.** The title is ready for "Begin a new life" in about half the time, and the
  new world is made while you choose who to be, so Begin opens your first year at once instead of after several seconds.

- **The Marshal's seat is optional.** A ruler may keep a Marshal in peace: Aldmark starts with Leofric Stane in the
  seat. A Marshal named for a war still stands down when it ends. As ruler you may name a General or a proven commander
  Marshal at any time, but not just anyone.
- **Every command has one holder.** Captains of companies and battalions, the Grand Masters of the orders, your staff,
  your free company, the heads of houses and the court titles each have exactly one. A captain you replace is "once
  Captain" and no longer shares his company's name with the next man; the senior captain you displace takes your old
  company; a councillor cannot serve on your staff; an order whose Grand Master dies names another at once.

## v2.1.0-alpha.5 (2026-11-14 18:49 ET) · v2.1 foundations: posts, bodies, honest cards, war at your rank

- **Fairer endings.** A long life that was really a saint's, a scholar's or a keeper of secrets' is remembered for that,
  not just its purse: "The Wealthy" now comes after them.
- **Odds that add up.** Every line in the odds popover adds up to the number on the choice, even for a near-sure thing.
- **Scenes on their dates, fixed.** A scene that only mentions a month as a plan ("she means to cross the passes in June")
  is no longer held back until that month.

- **No war score.** A war now reads as the land each side holds and the great battles won, not a number. The army
  templates are gone from the War Room.
- **War at your rank.** A soldier sees his own corner of the war, a captain his front, a general the whole theatre. Only
  the crown, the Marshal, a commander or a captain in the field opens the War Room. Enemy numbers are guesses below a
  commander. A child has no Arms page, and you hear only of the elite orders news would bring you.
- **No more "initiative".** The pool of free days went with the new Next month and Next year buttons, but its words stayed:
  "60.0 initiative" on the Activities line, "Spend your initiative", "needs 2 initiative" on a market trip, a ⧗ on every
  tile. They are gone. An activity takes days; the Activities line says how tired you are (a little tired, weary, driven
  too hard), and a market trip that is too long says so in days.
- **A feast comes on its day.** The Spring Equinox no longer falls in August, and a scene that opens on "1 May" or on a fair
  is dealt only in that month. "Go to a feast" is shut in a month with none.
- **A fight says what it can end in.** A choice that opens a duel says "a fight to the death" or "a fight, not to the death",
  and a hunt says the beast may kill you.
- **A sick body does not read "hale".** While a fever is on you the health word is "ill with marsh fever".
- **The ending fits the life.** "Master of the Craft" is for a craft; "Survivor" only for a life that began in childhood.
- **One unit.** The stories speak kilometres, as the map and the roads do. No quiet month says the same thing twice in a
  year. The love philtre you could slip into someone's cup is gone.
- **Bodies that make sense.** Strength, speed, endurance, reflexes, wits and will are now a word for where you stand
  among every grown man and woman of your people, measured across the world: most people are "average", a labourer or
  soldier is above it, one in fifty is at either extreme and one in a thousand is off the chart. A child reads as a child
  ("strong for a child of nine"). Another people is also measured against men: a giant's strength is off the chart, and so is
  a giant's slowness. Click a row to see what it is measured against.
- **No more kilograms and milliseconds.** The body is described in words: no "lifts 120 kg", "7.2 m/s", "28 km a day"
  or "reactions 170 ms", and your height is "tall" or "a little short", not centimetres.
- **A skill needs a body (or a mind) to carry it.** Each step of a skill from Journeyman asks a modest baseline of its two
  stats (a Master about an average body or mind for that skill, never a great one). Below it the skill stalls and works
  as the lower step: a sword master with feeble reflexes fights as an Expert. Past it, build your character as you like.
  The world's masters all clear their baselines.
- **The odds you see are the odds that happen.** A check's odds now count the days your will carries a lost cause and the
  days a lapse throws a sure thing, so a long shot shown as a long shot comes up as often as it says.
- "Blades" is called **Swordsmanship** again (it still covers knives and a blade in each hand).
- **Longer lives.** A healthy grown person at peace now dies of nothing much far more rarely, gets over a fever or the
  flux more often, and mostly lives into the sixties or seventies; serfs and slaves a little less, the well fed a little
  more. Plague, wounds, beasts and war still kill.
- **One holder per post.** Every council seat, shire office and army or division command has exactly one holder. A
  promotion hands over a real command: a new General takes an army (its old general moves to the division you left),
  and nobody keeps two seats. Death, prison, deserting or resigning leave every post at once; walking away from a
  command in wartime costs you at court.
- **The Marshal.** In peace the Marshal's seat stands empty and a General leads. When a war needs one (two armies in
  the field, or two enemies at once) the crown names its best General, who takes the main host; at peace he stands
  down. You can be named Marshal only as a General, and then you command every army.
- No more made-up Marshals: above a General stands the real Marshal or the crown. A councillor who loses his seat is
  "once Chancellor", not a second Chancellor; dead lords no longer hold offices for months; new realms get a council.
- Older saves are set straight when they load, with a line for each thing fixed.
- **A Profile page for your character.** The first page of You: your whole standing figure, a card with your name, estate
  and rank, a line about you, and what you are worth in words (body, purse, name, honour); your strength, speed and mind on
  a scale of seven words; and folded sections for your titles, posts and offices (and the ones you have left), your skills
  and fighting style, renown and deeds, house and household, gear and heirlooms, years, health and scars. Press any word to
  see what it means. The rail's Full profile button opens it.
- **Arms that look made.** Every weapon and shield a person carries is drawn as the thing it is: an arming sword, a
  longsword, a falchion or a sabre; a bearded axe, a flanged mace, a war hammer; a spear, a halberd, a bill, a pike; a
  round shield, a kite, a targe or a buckler. A crude blade keeps its hammer marks and a cord grip, a fine one has a
  wire grip and its maker's mark, a master's is chased and gilt. Elves, dwarves, orcs and the desert peoples carry their
  own smiths' work.
- **Bows and crossbows are on the figure.** An archer's bow is slung on the back with its quiver, a crossbow with its case
  of bolts; an axe, mace or hammer hangs from the belt; a pole arm stands in the hand.

## v2.1.0-alpha.4 (2026-11-13 15:01 ET) · New faces and a calmer screen

- **New character art everywhere.** Every person in the world is drawn in the new illustrated style, from the
  bondman to the king, with a face of their own that children inherit from their parents. A face and body maker at the
  start of a life: shape every feature, pick hair, beard and build, or let it surprise you.
- **A calmer screen.** The page you are on gets most of the width; the story waits behind a Story button; six
  activities to start with and the rest one click away; the market, laws and rosters fold until you open them.

## v2.1.0-alpha.3 (2026-11-13 02:40 ET) · The game's own code made readable

- Nothing changes in play: the game is now built from readable source instead of the packed release file, proven to
  run exactly the same. Every later fix and feature is easier and safer because of it.

## v2.1.0-alpha.2 (2026-11-13 01:35 ET) · Foundations: lighter steps, click-to-open, Next month or Next year

- Descriptions open when you click or tap, never on hover; long explanations sit behind a small ⓘ. An activity
  shows what it is, what it costs and how it may go before you commit, and its result is never cut short.
- Next month is much lighter: the page redraws once per press, the story log updates in place, and a long life no
  longer gets slower. Starting a life shows a progress line.
- Fixes: the duel's hint about your foe's move is right; the Adapting bar uses words; a passed-over heir is told why;
  phone buttons and person rows are the right size; no two-year-old sits on a royal council.
- **Next month or Next year.** Two buttons sit in fixed places, on a phone and on a desktop, and neither ever turns into
  a stop button. A year runs month by month and stops early only for something that matters (a real decision, a call
  to war, an illness, a birth or death at home, coming of age), saying why; everyday chatter waits for the end of the
  step. Childhood moves by the year.
- **No more initiative.** Do as much as you like; do too much and you grow weary, then hurt.
- A phone's clock now shows your age ("Age 9 · March 1105").
- Every screen draws faster again (a slowdown that came with Next year is gone).

## v2.1.0-alpha.1 (2026-11-12 11:20 ET) · Round 2 begins

- The game no longer slows down after you have seen a hint or lived on as an heir: a month late in a long life
  costs about half what it did.
- A court bug that could leave two lords on one seat for the rest of a game is fixed.
- "Would teach you f" and "1 armies in the field" now read properly.

- Nothing changes in play yet: the game's files were reorganised so the work ahead (lag, ranks, click-to-open
  descriptions, a cleaner screen, real households, real-world names, distinct faces, a detailed map, real military
  branches and deeper war) has a clear home.

## v2.0.0-beta.1 (Nov 6)

- Cards tell you what is at stake, including when a failure can kill you.
- War by rank: a soldier hears rough numbers and rumours, a general sees exact ones; wars you are not in stay rumour.
- Kingdoms have real names; families and clans make sense; bodies are judged against people their own age.
- Words instead of 0–100 numbers; map, layout and phone fixes; wars come at a sensible pace; advisors no longer lose
  their turn on scenes you never see.

## v2.0.0-alpha.1 to alpha.10 (Nov 1–5)

- One generated continent replaces the two maps. One "Next month" button; months bring cards one at a time and quiet
  months a short note. Bodies in real units (kilograms, metres a second). Three generations of family, and house law
  for succession. Courts from dukes down to lords. Relations between realms as named reasons. You see only your own
  war, at your rank. Saves carry all of it.

## v1.x (Oct 12 – Nov 1)

- The shipped release, then rounds of fixes and features on top of it: the chronicle, honest odds, sieges, named
  squads, schemes, story arcs, realm customs, the redesign (Woodblock & Light), journeys, the atlas, chapters, share
